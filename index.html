About my work

Passionate about building things that matter.

I'm Shubharthak Sangharsha, an Applied AI & Machine Learning Engineer based in Adelaide, Australia. I hold a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning from the University of Adelaide.

I specialize in autonomous robotics, action-conditioned world models, LLMs, computer vision, and scalable production AI web applications deployed on AWS. My background encompasses hands-on research on Unitree Go2 quadruped robots, production full-stack systems at devshubh.me, and health-tech software engineering at Curve Tomorrow.

I believe the best technology is invisible — it just works, and works beautifully.
